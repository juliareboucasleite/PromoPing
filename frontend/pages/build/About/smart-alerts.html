Smart Alerts

Alert Types

  • Price Alert: Notification when the price drops below your set threshold
  • Stock Alert: Notice when a product is back in stock after being sold out
  • Offer Alert: Notification of special promotions and temporary discounts
  • Increase Alert: Notice when the price rises significantly

Notification Channels

Email

Receive alerts directly in your inbox. Set up your email in the notification preferences and receive updates whenever prices change.

Discord

Add our Discord bot to your servers and receive real-time notifications in your channels. Ideal for those who want to stay up to date without constantly checking email.

How to Set Up Alerts

  1. Go to your notification preferences in the dashboard
  2. Choose the desired notification channels (Email or Discord)
  3. Set the price threshold for each monitored product
  4. Configure the alert frequency (instant, daily, weekly)
  5. Enable alerts for the products you want to monitor

Customising Alerts

Customise your alerts according to your needs:

  • Price Threshold: Set the target price for each product
  • Frequency: Choose to receive instant alerts or periodic summaries
  • Filters: Configure alerts only for specific products or categories
  • Schedule: Set preferred hours to receive notifications

Manage Alerts

Have full control over your alerts:

  • Enable or disable alerts for specific products
  • Temporarily pause all notifications
  • View the history of received alerts
  • Set up bulk alerts for multiple products
