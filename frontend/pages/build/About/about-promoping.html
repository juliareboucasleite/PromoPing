About PromoPing

What is PromoPing?

PromoPing is a specialised platform designed to help you monitor and compare product prices in a single place. It works as a smart tool that gathers data from multiple online stores, giving users the ability to track prices, receive alerts when discounts appear, and make smarter buying decisions.

Why was it created?

PromoPing was built to help shoppers save money on their online purchases. With prices constantly rising and the difficulty of keeping up with promotions across multiple stores, PromoPing simplifies the process, letting users always find the best deals without wasting time searching manually across dozens of different sites.

Our mission is to help people shop smarter, saving time and money, and making sure they never miss the best offer available on the market.
