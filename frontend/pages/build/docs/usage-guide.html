Usage Guide

Getting started with PromoPing

Monitor prices across multiple online stores. In the dashboard you have your product list, alerts, and quick actions.

Add Products

Click "Add Product", paste the store URL, and set the target price and notifications. You can also enter the name, store, and prices manually.

Alerts and Notifications

Use price drop or daily summary alerts via email or Discord. Configure the channels in your preferences.

Manage Products

In the list you can see the current price, history, and alert status. Use multi-select to update thresholds, enable or disable alerts, export PDFs, or delete items.

Tips

Realistic thresholds, such as 10-20% below the current price, and tracking the same product across multiple stores usually produce the best results.
