Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions

What is PromoPing?

PromoPing is a price-monitoring platform that lets you track products across multiple online stores and receive automatic notifications when prices drop, helping users save money on their purchases.

How does the monitoring system work?

PromoPing periodically checks product prices in supported stores. When a price reaches the target defined by the user, the system sends notifications through Discord or email.

Which stores are supported?

PromoPing supports more than 20 stores, including Worten, FNAC, Continente, Pingo Doce, IKEA, Radio Popular, Auchan, PcDiga, Amazon, and many others. The system automatically detects the store from the product URL.

Account and Authentication

How do I create an account?

You can create an account through the standard email/password sign-up flow, or use social sign-in with Google OAuth. Open the sign-up page and follow the instructions.

I forgot my password. How do I recover it?

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password" and follow the instructions sent by email. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder.

Can I use social sign-in?

Yes. PromoPing supports sign-in with Google OAuth and Discord. Just click the corresponding button on the sign-in page.

Plans and Limits

What plans are available?

PromoPing offers 4 plans:

  • Free: 5 products, checks every 24h
  • Basic: 25 products, checks every 4h
  • Standard: 100 products, checks every 30min
  • Premium: 500 products, checks every 5min

Can I change my plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time from the plans page in the dashboard. Changes are applied immediately.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

If you exceed the product limit, you will be notified and can upgrade your plan or remove some products. The system will continue monitoring the products within the limit.

Notifications

How do I configure notifications?

In the dashboard, go to "Preferences" and configure the notification channels you want to use (Discord or email). You can customize the frequency and alert types.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check whether:

  • Notifications are enabled in your preferences
  • Your email is correct and verified
  • The Discord bot has been added to the server
  • Notifications are not going to your spam folder

Can I temporarily disable notifications?

Yes, you can pause notifications in your preferences or disable notifications for specific products.

Technical Problems

The site does not load. What should I do?

Check whether:

  • You have an internet connection
  • The server is online (check the service status page)
  • Your browser is up to date
  • Temporarily disable antivirus or firewall software

Prices are not updating. Why?

This can happen because of:

  • Temporary issues with the stores
  • Changes to the structure of store pages
  • Rate limiting restrictions
  • Connectivity problems

The system automatically tries again in the next cycle.

How do I report a bug?

You can report bugs through the support page or by sending an email to suporte@promoping.pt. Include details about the issue and screenshots if possible.
