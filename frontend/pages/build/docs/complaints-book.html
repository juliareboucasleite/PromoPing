Electronic Complaints Book

1. Availability of the Complaints Book

In compliance with Decree-Law No. 74/2017 of June 21, PromoPing makes the Complaints Book available to users and customers in electronic format.

Open the Electronic Complaints Book ->

2. How to Submit a Complaint

  1. Aceda ao portal oficial em www.livroreclamacoes.pt.
  2. Select the "Submit Complaint" option.
  3. Identifique a entidade reclamada como PromoPing (NIF 307687848).
  4. Describe the facts that led to the complaint and submit the form.

The complaint is automatically sent to the competent regulatory authority and to the company, which has a legal deadline of 15 business days to respond.

3. Direct Resolution

Before using the Complaints Book, you may contact us directly. Many issues can be resolved quickly through suporte@promoping.pt.

4. Alternative Dispute Resolution

If you prefer to contact an Alternative Consumer Dispute Resolution (ADR) entity, see our Alternative Dispute Resolution page.
