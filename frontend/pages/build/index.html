Price monitoring for Portuguese online stores

Save smarter. Track every drop.

PromoPing is a price monitoring service for Portuguese online stores. Simply paste a product link, set the price you wish to pay, and we will notify you automatically by email or Discord as soon as the price drops.

What you can do with PromoPing

  • Monitor products from Portuguese retailers
  • Set alerts by price or discount
  • Receive automatic notifications by email or Discord

Get alerts on Discord, by email, or both

PromoPing tracks the price of any product you add and notifies you the moment it drops. Pick the channel that suits you, Discord for real-time pings, email for a quieter inbox notification, or both at once on paid plans. Every alert is also waiting in your dashboard.

Setup takes under a minute. Create your account, paste the product links you want to follow, and choose how often we check, from once a day on the free plan up to every hour on Premium. No browser extension, no software to install.

Bringing PromoPing to Discord? Add our bot to your own server to receive alerts there, or join the PromoPing community server to chat with other deal-hunters.
