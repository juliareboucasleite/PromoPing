Usage Guide

Getting Started with PromoPing

Overview

PromoPing is a comprehensive price monitoring platform that helps you track products across multiple online stores. This guide will walk you through all the essential features and how to use them effectively.

Dashboard Overview

When you first log in, you'll see the main dashboard with:

  • Product List: All your monitored products
  • Price Alerts: Recent price changes and notifications
  • Analytics: Price trends and savings statistics
  • Quick Actions: Add new products, manage alerts

Adding Products to Monitor

Method 1: Direct URL

The easiest way to add a product is by pasting its URL:

  1. Click the "Add Product" button
  2. Paste the product URL from your favorite store
  3. PromoPing will automatically detect the product details
  4. Set your desired price threshold
  5. Choose notification preferences

Method 2: Manual Entry

For more control, you can manually enter product information:

Product Name: iPhone 15 Pro
Store: Apple Store
Current Price: $999.00
Target Price: $899.00
Category: Electronics

Setting Up Price Alerts

Configure alerts to be notified when prices drop:

Alert Types

  • Price Drop: Notify when price goes below threshold
  • Price Increase: Alert when price rises significantly
  • Stock Alert: Notify when out-of-stock items become available
  • Daily Summary: Receive daily price updates

Notification Channels

Choose how you want to receive alerts:

  • Email notifications
  • WhatsApp messages
  • Discord bot notifications
  • In-app notifications

Managing Your Products

Product List View

Your product list shows:

  • Product name and image
  • Current price and price history
  • Store information
  • Alert status
  • Last updated timestamp

Bulk Actions

Perform actions on multiple products:

• Select multiple products
• Update price thresholds
• Enable/disable alerts
• Export to CSV
• Delete selected items

Analytics and Reports

Price History

View detailed price trends for each product:

  • Interactive price charts
  • Historical data export
  • Price prediction algorithms
  • Seasonal trend analysis

Savings Reports

Track your savings over time:

  • Total money saved
  • Best deals found
  • Average discount percentage
  • Monthly savings summary

Advanced Features

Price Comparison

Compare prices across multiple stores:

  1. Add the same product from different stores
  2. View side-by-side price comparison
  3. Get alerts for the best deals
  4. Track price differences over time

Wishlist Management

Organize your products into wishlists:

  • Create custom wishlists (e.g., "Electronics", "Gifts")
  • Set different alert preferences per wishlist
  • Share wishlists with family and friends
  • Get budget alerts for wishlist totals

Tips for Best Results

  • Set realistic price thresholds (10-20% below current price)
  • Monitor multiple stores for the same product
  • Use seasonal trends to time your purchases
  • Enable stock alerts for popular items
  • Regularly review and update your product list
